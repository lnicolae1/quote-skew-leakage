Committed convention: SPREAD = sum v_j*(p_j - S_j) direct, INV = PnL - spread residual,
PnL = cash_T + q_T*S_final. v_j = sgn*size, sgn=+1 when the MM sold.
8 seeds x 604800s (7.00 days), MM present, k=0.17763 C=13.66 gamma=1.3392e-06.
lam=1.8040 p_market=0.0126 disp=0.0055, JOIN clipping. Arms this run: life=60

With INV as a residual, INV verifies nothing; every error in the
computation lands there by construction.

Gate 2 and Gate 3 are both near-tautological. Gate 2 holds because
cash is benchmark-independent; Gate 3 holds because p cancels:
SPREAD(V) - SPREAD(lag) = sum sgn*sz*(S_lag - V) identically, so it
passes unless a field name is mistyped. Neither is evidence the legs
Are right. The two genuinely independent checks here are:
  (i)  Self-test T4, against the committed lifetime_sweep.run()
  (ii) The wrong-side fraction against staleness.py's committed
       75.09% +/- 0.39%; a different script, different
       bookkeeping, same population. This one halts on failure.

quantities, so the mid[] array feeding SPREAD(now) is unvalidated by
it. mid[ti] = last is unconditional in lifetime_sweep.py:147 (the
continue at :151 gates the book-stats block, not the mid array) and
the transcription here matches. Read from the file, not assumed.
