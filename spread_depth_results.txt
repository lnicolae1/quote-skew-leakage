  Clean samples with >1000 ask levels: 357646 (50.348%)
  >>> Beyond-cap levels are present in this dataset (levels/side exceeded 1000 at
      least once). Those excess levels can only sit deep in the book; a snapshot
      anchor can never itself contain more than 1000 levels/side, so anything past
      level 1000 was built entirely from diff-stream additions accumulating since
      the last (re)connect. Practically, this means the far-tail bucket (100+bp)
      is the one most likely to be contaminated by this beyond-cap accumulation --
      it may not represent 'real' resting depth as cleanly as a full independent
      snapshot would, since it's a mix of originally-snapshotted levels and levels
      added later purely via diffs with no independent cross-check beyond rank 1000
      (per the verified fact: never compare reconstructed depth to a snapshot past
      rank 1000). The near-touch buckets (0-1bp .. 50-100bp) are NOT affected --
      they sit well inside the 1000-level cap essentially always.
