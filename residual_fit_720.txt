8. Limitations
==============================================================================================================================
  L1 The adverse move is a mid-to-mid change (staleness.py:156). V enters only through d, so
     an intercept is a residual in mid-drift terms, not in V terms.
  L2 The bucket threshold is per seed. The 40.8723 figure in staleness_results.txt is the
     across-seed mean of eight different thresholds; using it as a single scalar would not
     reproduce 6.93. Task 2B carries seed s's own life=720 threshold to seed s.
  L3 e0 is not observed. It is proxied by the sd of the full 1-second mid change, an upper
     bound, so var(e0)/var(d) and the implied bias are upper bounds.
  L4 Neither construction is unbiased. m0 shares its error with the outcome (intercept biased
     DOWN); S_now removes that but carries the maker's own fill impact (intercept biased UP).
     The bracketing claim is about sign only. The S_now arm is gated on the measured
     contamination in section 6 rather than trusted.
  L5 The octile x-axis is unweighted while the y-axis is SIZE-WEIGHTED. That is the committed
     convention (staleness.py:168 vs :173-176), preserved so the table reproduces; it is NOT
     the convention the primary fit uses.
  L6 No instance wrapper is used, so no wrapper-inertness claim is made. The guarantee is T4
     against the committed lifetime_sweep.run(), plus Gate 1's thirteen reproduced figures.
  L7 Task 2B's arms differ in book structure as well as in staleness (lifetime_sweep_results
     section C: 52.2 levels/side at life=60 against 556.9 at life=720, and only life=720 is
     in-band on levels). A cross-arm comparison is therefore NOT ceteris paribus.

  No committed default changed. Nothing retuned. No sweep. No repricing. JOIN clipping rule
  untouched. No sniffer.
