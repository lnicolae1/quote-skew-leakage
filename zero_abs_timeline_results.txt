D. Which story survives
======================================================================================================================
  *** The verdict does NOT REST on NEVER_SEEN. An earlier version of
  this block concluded from NEVER_SEEN/diff, and that quantity is
  history-contaminated: ever_seen accumulates monotonically, so once
  a price has been visited any later zero for it scores RE_DELETE
  instead. NEVER_SEEN therefore measures new price territory; how
  much of the price grid this run has not yet covered; and it
  decays as the run proceeds regardless of what the market does.
  The 07-12 column shows the decay directly: 0.2046 at 00:00 falling
  to 0.0005 by 17:00, three orders of magnitude in one day, with no
  corresponding move in CREATED/diff. See zero_abs_probe.py's
  correction block. ***

  The verdict rests on ZERO_ABS/diff and ZERO_ABS/CREATED, which do
  not depend on bucket assignment. A ZERO_ABS is a ZERO_ABS whether
  it lands in RE_DELETE or NEVER_SEEN.
