D. The verdict
============================================================================================================================
  Real book has 0.5x the levels within 1bp that the sim does (1.57 against 3.28).
  Sim level spacing is 0.6x the real book's ($0.7233 against $1.2000).
  Real spacing is 120.0 ticks; sim spacing is 72.3 ticks.

  Diagnosis REFUTED, and in the opposite direction. The SIM is the denser book near the
  touch; 3.28 levels/1bp against the real 1.57, at $0.7233 spacing against $1.2000. Near-touch
  sparsity therefore cannot explain impact that is worse in the sim than in reality, and
  the mechanism paragraph in 0860f69 is wrong. The real Binance.US book genuinely carries
  only ~1.6 price levels per side within $6.20, spaced ~120 ticks apart: it is a very thin
  venue, and an intuition that a small trade should not move it was calibrated to a dense
  equity book rather than to this one.
  Limits: one real day, not eleven; level density is far more stable than volatility but
  this is a single day. Crossed-book samples skipped. Nothing retuned, no committed
  default changed; this measures the gap, it does not close it.
