4. lam holding levels/side at the life=720 value (556.9), per life
====================================================================================================
  model: levels ~ lam*(1-p_market)*life; at fixed p_market
  lam needed at lifetime L = lam_held * (target_levels / levels(L))  (lam_lin)

  lam_sub (speculative bound, not conservative) applies
  section 2's LIFETIME exponent (0.9330) to lam: lam_needed =
  lam_held * (target/levels)**(1/0.9330); assumes lam and lifetime saturate alike
  untested: lifetime saturates as orders are consumed or expire before the book fills,
  lam adds fresh orders, so lam may be closer to linear than lifetime; direction unknown
