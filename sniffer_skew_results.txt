========================================================================================================================
Skew sniffer, undefended baseline (left endpoint)
========================================================================================================================
Working point: lam=1.8040 p_market=0.0126 mean_lifetime=720s disp=0.0055, JOIN clipping. MM present.
15 seeds x 86400s per k. gamma=DEFAULT_GAMMA=1e-06 (not chosen). DEFAULT_K untouched. horizon=86400s.
Time split 70/30, out-of-sample only, R2 against the test-set mean.

Sniffer sees: MM bid/ask, book mid, clock, (T-t), public prints.
Not visible to it: inventory, cash, PnL, counterparties, MM internal state.
Adversary knows gamma, sigma and (T-t).

skew = (MM_bid + MM_ask)/2 - observed_mid.  C = gamma*sigma^2*(T-t) varies:
(T-t) decays within the test slice, which depresses raw-skew OLS;
normalized feature skew/(mid^2*tau) is the one to compare
with the predicted R2 = 1.0.

  k=0.16230 measured in 119s  (kept 84672 of 86400 samples after the tau floor)
  k=0.12429 measured in 113s  (kept 84672 of 86400 samples after the tau floor)
