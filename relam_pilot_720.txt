Pre-registered PASS conditions; fixed before any cell ran
======================================================================================================================
  P-cal   all four scored targets MET, using clipped_window's own band()/verdict(). MET requires the
          whole 95% interval inside the band; straddling an edge is UNRESOLVED and is NOT a hit.
          At 3 seeds this is a hard bar and UNRESOLVED is a likely outcome. It is not weakened.
  P-edge  E[d] < h with the gap clearing 2.0 SE, paired per seed (the seeds share the V path).
  P-PART  mm_fill_share >= 5.0% and mm_near_share >= 15.0%.
          These are a judgement call, at roughly half the committed point's own k=0.17763 values
          (8.63% of fills, 31.29% of near-touch size). Half, because a re-solve moving lam up to 12x
          should not have to leave participation untouched; and not lower, because a maker under
          5% of fills and 15% of near-touch size is not the venue's liquidity provider in any sense
          that makes the leakage question meaningful. A bar a ghost could clear would let a cell pass
          by making the maker irrelevant, which is exactly what this condition exists to catch.

  A cell survives only if all three hold. Survivors are not ranked and no winner is picked.
  If no cell survives, that is a bounded negative result: the (lam, mean_lifetime) lever is exhausted.

  Burden asymmetry, flagged not fixed: P-cal and P-edge are interval tests; P-PART is a point-estimate
  test, because its thresholds were pre-registered as bare percentages. The 95% interval is printed
  beside each participation figure so the reader can see whether the point estimate is resolved.
