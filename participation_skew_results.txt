==============================================================================================================================
Does skewing control inventory?; three paired arms at fixed C, over the clean lever range
==============================================================================================================================
Working point: lam=1.8040 p_market=0.0126 mean_lifetime=720s disp=0.0055, JOIN clipping.
k=0.17763. gamma_cap=9.37e-06 (C=13.66, the binding gate-2 viability cap).
5 seeds x 86400s, paired on identical seeds. H = 3600s fixed. Train slice = first 70%.
DEFAULT_GAMMA=1e-06 and DEFAULT_QUOTE_SIZE=0.02 untouched; both are constructor arguments.

  Skew    gamma_cap, normal AS quoting.
  FLAT    gamma_cap, reservation price forced to the mid. Same width, same flow, no skew.
          Primary control; R_skew - R_flat is computed against this.
  GAMMA0  gamma=1e-09, the literal 'set gamma = 0'. Removes the skew and narrows the quote,
          so it is NOT a valid control. Retained because the FLAT-GAMMA0 gap is the spread
          confound, measured rather than argued.

Scope: quote_size is clean only to 0.02 BTC, so this spans 6.1% -> 13.8% volume share --
entirely minority. The minority-vs-majority contrast is unreachable on a calibrated book
(non-MM liquidity is the calibration) and is dropped as a claim. R_skew - R_flat below is a
Bound over a 2.3x span, not evidence of a participation gradient in either direction.

1. Is inventory reverting because of skewing, or because of flow mixing?  -- the load-bearing result
==============================================================================================================================
   quote_size   vol share     phi_skew     phi_flat   phi_gamma0      half-life skew      half-life FLAT
        0.005       6.11%     0.996122     0.996942     0.997374       12834s (3.6h)       15168s (4.2h)
        0.010       8.88%     0.995135     0.995202     0.995401       11817s (3.3h)       15691s (4.4h)
        0.020      13.33%     0.995982     0.996500     0.993931       30944s (8.6h)       10440s (2.9h)

  phi_flat is the lag-1 inventory autocorrelation with the MM quoting the same width but
  not skewing. phi_flat ~ 1 means inventory is a random walk with no restoring force, so
  whatever reversion the skewing MM shows is attributable to skewing. phi_flat materially
  below 1 means flow mixing reverts inventory on its own, and the sd(q)-vs-gamma
  insensitivity reported in d5d134c has nothing to do with participation.

==============================================================================================================================
What is dropped, and why it is a result rather than a failure
==============================================================================================================================
  The minority-participant claim is withdrawn. Majority participation is unreachable on a
  calibrated book because non-MM liquidity is the calibration, so the old book's 64% volume
  share was a symptom of the mis-scaling the clipped recalibration fixed. The 1.3x-vs-7x
  sd(q) contrast conflates participation with CALIBRATION and no lever decouples them on one
  book. That is a methodological result about this class of experiment, and it is the honest
  thing to record in place of the claim it retires.
