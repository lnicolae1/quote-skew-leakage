======================================================================================================================
Limitations
======================================================================================================================
  G1  At low gamma the hidden quantity is smaller:
      sd(skew) scales with gamma;
      at gamma 1e-6 sd(skew) = $0.02724,
      2.7 ticks ($0.01 tick).
      Below a tick the quote centre does not move
      and a null would be uninterpretable,
      so no gamma below 1e-6.
  G2  Three points, not a sweep; no shape against
      gamma is claimed.
  G3  Non-adaptive smoothing attacker, as in arm B: every
      R2 is an upper bound on defense performance.
  G4  Gate 2 caps gamma at 9.40e-06 for spread realism; nothing
      above the cap is run.
  G5  Defenses not calibrated to each other
      (JITTER one sd of skew, QUANT 0.34 sd of inventory).
      Only shapes in M are compared and scored;
      each defense is calibrated to itself
      across gammas.
  G6  No committed module or default modified; both defenses
      are the phase7 classes, passed
      in through run()'s mm_override.
