==============================================================================================================================
What this run does not do, and what limits it
==============================================================================================================================
  L1  It does not measure damage. It measures the range over which a damage measurement
      could be made without decalibrating the book. Phase 7 is not run.
  L2  It scores against the real-data targets (clipped_window.TARGETS), not against the
      simulator's MM-absent output. On levels/side those references differ a lot; 666.34
      against 551.24; so this column reads differently from the probe's by construction.
      The MM-absent simulator sits only 3.4% above the levels band floor of 533.07 before
      quote_size does anything, so a levels miss here would not be attributable to the maker.
  L3  Near-touch depth is NOT one of the four scored targets and is not scored. The probe
      reported it; clipped_window never scored it. Adding it as a fifth target would be a
      new gate, and new gates are not invented inside a run.
  L4  The MM is a large fraction of its own near-touch measurement bucket at these sizes
      (the probe measured 4.4% at 0.050 and 19.8% at 0.200). A size that passes the four
      targets can still be rebuilding the near-touch book rather than trading into it.
  L5  T=86400s per cell, so sd(q) is measured over one day while the damage A/B runs seven.
      Inventory half-lives here are 3-9h, so a day contains only a few, and sd(q) at 7 days
      could be larger. That would make this test conservative about reaching the threshold.
  L6  gamma is held at the binding viability cap. sd(q) moves only 1.33x across five decades
      of gamma, so this is not a material free parameter; but it is held, not swept.
  L7  No committed default changed, probe_quotesize_lever.py was not modified, and the
      measurement loop is asserted identical to the probe's by T-mirror.
