==============================================================================
Is lambda actually immune to the repair? -- proven, not assumed
==============================================================================
  The cache's lambda was written by regime_scan.py (control arm, before any
  repair existed). The results file's lambda was written by the REPAIRED
  arm. If the repair cannot touch the trade stream these must be identical.

  The results file prints lambda to 5 decimal places, so the repaired value
  is compared at that precision; a tighter tolerance would only be testing
  my own parsing, not the data. Full cache precision is shown alongside.

  day          control (full)     repaired (5dp) identical 
  2026-08-17       0.026944814565        0.02694 yes       
  2026-08-18       0.017962978368        0.01796 yes       
  2026-08-19       0.085568553856        0.08557 yes       
  2026-08-20       0.103970182861        0.10397 yes       
  2026-08-21       0.138078848402        0.13808 yes       
  2026-08-22       0.075301538392        0.07530 yes       

  All six identical at the printed precision. Lambda is untouched by the
  repair, so it is a genuinely independent axis on which to test the
  recovered volatilities.

  Having proven they agree, the full-precision cache lambda is used for the
  regression below, so no rounding from the results file enters the fit.
  Note: the recovered volatilities are read from the results file at 2 dp
  (e.g. 25.87%), so each carries up to 0.005 pp of print rounding. Against
  a residual SD near 4 pp that is ~0.1% of one SD and changes nothing.

==============================================================================
(5) CONTROL: the 40 clean days' own residual sign balance
==============================================================================
  An OLS fit forces residuals to sum to zero, but not to split evenly. If
  the residual distribution is skewed, most points sit on one side of the
  line, and that; not 0.5; is the null the six must be judged against.

  LAMBDA fit  clean days above the line: 22/40 = 55.0%   median residual +0.822 pp
  SIZE fit    clean days above the line: 20/40 = 50.0%   median residual +0.286 pp

(3) Sign test on the six; both fits, side by side
==============================================================================
  fit          six positive   p vs 0.5         p vs clean rate 
  LAMBDA       6 of 6         0.0156           0.0277          
  SIZE         6 of 6         0.0156           0.0156          

  Caveat on every p ABOVE: the six are consecutive days and daily
  volatility has lag-1 autocorrelation +0.4370 (regime_scan_results.txt),
  so they are nowhere near six independent draws. A crude AR(1) adjustment
  n_eff = n(1-rho)/(1+rho) gives n_eff = 2.35, i.e. roughly two independent
  observations. Treat these p-values as an upper bound on the evidence,
  not as a test that clears any threshold.
