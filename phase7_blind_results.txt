==============================================================================================================================
Limitations
==============================================================================================================================
  L1  Non-adaptive sniffer (committed OLS; no boundary-crossing
      detector, no smoother). Transition times would localize q
      at bucket boundaries; that attack is not implemented,
      so each R2 is an upper bound on defense
      performance
      and a lower bound
      on the leak.
  L2  Two widths, not a sweep (both pinned to committed
      numbers); the shape of R2 against W_Q
      is not measured.
  L3  T=86400s per arm, one day. Inventory half-lives on this book
      are 3-9h; sd(q) and
      R_direct could differ over seven days.
  L4  gamma held at the viability cap, not swept (sd(q) moves
      1.33x across five decades of gamma).
  L5  Cost in dollars not stated while the control
      arm loses money at 3.23 SE; R_direct is an
      inventory-dynamics quantity.
  L6  No committed module or default modified; the defense is
      a subclass.
