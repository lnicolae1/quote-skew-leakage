======================================================================================================================
Limitations
======================================================================================================================
  L1  Non-adaptive attacker: fixed window,
      no bucket-boundary detection (the best
      response to quantization;
      not implemented).
      Every R2 here is an upper bound on how well each defense
      works against a real adversary.
  L2  Defenses not calibrated to equal strength:
      JITTER at one sd of skew and QUANT at 0.34 sd of inventory
      different amounts of hiding; levels of the two
      curves not comparable, only their shapes in M.
      only shape is scored.
  L3  Trailing mean uses a running sum over up to
      86400 rows (floating-point drift vs a
      pairwise or Kahan sum); M=1 is
      special-cased to the identity so the control
      demand bit-equality.
  L4  At large M the smoothed skew columns are highly collinear;
      the committed ols skips singular pivots,
      so a large-M fit may use fewer
      effective columns.
  L5  sigma_eps derived at the time-averaged tau; realized
      noise-to-signal is better than designed early in the
      day and worse late; the regression pools both.
      M/(M+1) prediction inherits that approximation.
  L6  No committed module or default modified; both defenses
      are subclasses.
      does with FlatQuoteMM.
