!! Binance publishes [price, AGGREGATE_QUANTITY]; two fields. Verified at the raw feed.
!! Size per order and orders per level therefore have no real counterpart and never will;
!! the information was never transmitted. They are reported sim-only and are never scored
!! or ratioed against anything real. A real level aggregates an unknown number of makers
!! while a sim level aggregates a known number of orders, so per-level is apples-to-apples
!! and per-order is not comparable even in principle.

!! Sides are never pooled in Section A. The real book is 1.83:1 asymmetric near the touch
!! (bid 0.0452 / ask 0.0247 BTC), so pooling the sim and ratioing it against each real side
!! separately would manufacture a ~1.8x spread between the two ratio rows out of the
!! asymmetry alone. Bid is compared to bid, ask to ask.

!! n counts level observations, not distinct levels. A level persisting across many scans
!! is counted once per scan, on both sides equally; a consistent time-weighted convention.

!! Percentiles: sorted[int(p*(n-1))], no interpolation, applied identically to both sides.
!! Real figures are pooled over samples and carry no error bar; there is one real book,
!! not fifteen draws of one. Do not read their digits as precision.

  Branch 1; confirmed by stacking_source.py. The sizes drawn are faithful and the excess is
    orders stacking on levels, which points at the JOIN clipping rule. That rule is recorded
    as measured but not adopted; nothing in the collected data validates it as what the real
    venue does. It was chosen because it made the book behave. Measured split: unclipped
    orders rest median 1 / mean 1.00216 / p95 1 per level; clipped orders median 8 / mean 21.3
    / p95 87. Every multi-order level in this book is a clipped level.
  Branch 2; dead. per-order does NOT run high at the draw: submitted/source is 1.00 at
    every percentile over 2,406,478 orders. trade_sizes.bin is not implicated by this number.
    (Open limitation #3; taker sizes standing in for maker sizes with no maker-size data
    anywhere behind them; is untouched by this and still stands.)
